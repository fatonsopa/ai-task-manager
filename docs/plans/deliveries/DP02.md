## Goal and specification

Deliver **DP02-E1 — Complete one real non-code task and report its accepted result to the lead**. Starting from the DP01 installation, an operator prepares fresh authorized work, runs it through the normal installed CLI worker and independent product reviewer, confirms the allowed Done result in CLI and Linear, and observes the exact result reaching the lead AI and its public user report.

The author task produces a small report from an owned deterministic dataset. Its expected totals and required work are fixed before execution. A separate approved reviewer task judges the actual artifact through the product's bound inspection/review path. A report-writing model's final answer alone cannot complete the author task.
