## Goal and specification

Deliver **DP03-E1 — Deliver one reviewed worker commit to the trusted dev target and report it**. A fresh code-bearing task changes a small independent fixture, passes its selected checks and guarded worker commit, receives the two required product reviews, merges through the product into local `refs/heads/dev`, confirms In Dev in CLI and Linear, and delivers the same result to the lead AI and user. Owned processes and resources are then retired or explicitly retained under their supported rules.

This extends the [DP02 result](https://github.com/fatonsopa/ai-task-manager/blob/c1a487740f136d5f4c91f95ea85d7a8ead99f4a2/docs/plans/deliveries/DP02-handoff.md), following the [delivery roadmap](../incremental-delivery-proposal.md). The [Spec Driven Development document (SDD)](../../spec_driven_development.md), [Policy (POL)](../../policy.md), [Test Plan (TP)](../../test_plan.md) and [requirements](../../../config/requirements.json) retain authority. Plan/epic tracking is [TST-241](https://linear.app/manaferra/issue/TST-241). Independent task IDs and native parent relationships remain separate.
